# Injection acceptance essentials

Derive horizontal and vertical acceptance from the injection scheme, incoming
emittances/Twiss/energy spread, offsets, kicker errors and required capture
efficiency. Millimetre apertures depend on launch optics; the incoming emittance
is not the stored equilibrium emittance. Use a stated, sourced provisional
scenario when injector details are unspecified.

Off-axis injection must accommodate septum separation and the incoming beam;
a nonlinear/pulsed-multipole kicker is not inherently on-axis. Swap-out can
accept smaller transverse apertures; longitudinal injection additionally needs
sufficient energy/phase acceptance and RF capture.

For scale, a [HEPS design study (2021)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s41365-021-00974-z)
reports about 1 mm DA in both planes for swap-out with injected emittances about
20/4 nm. This is a conditional example, not a universal aperture requirement.

Search primary literature for the chosen scheme's parameters and derive the
project's targets. Distinguish ideal DA and local momentum-acceptance results
from demonstrated injection capture; validate only the scope being claimed.
