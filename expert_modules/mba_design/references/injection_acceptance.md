# Injection acceptance essentials

Derive transverse acceptance from the injection scheme, incoming beam/Twiss,
offsets, kicker errors and capture efficiency. Millimetre apertures depend on
launch optics; incoming and equilibrium emittances differ. Unspecified injector
details may use sourced provisional scenarios, not additional hard requirements.

Off-axis injection needs septum/beam clearance; pulsed-multipole kickers are not
inherently on-axis. Swap-out may allow smaller DA; longitudinal injection needs
energy/phase acceptance and RF capture.

For scale, a [HEPS design study (2021)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s41365-021-00974-z)
reports about 1 mm DA in both planes for swap-out with injected emittances about
20/4 nm. This is a conditional example, not a universal aperture requirement.

Use primary literature for scenario parameters. Ideal DA and local MA do not
establish injection capture; validate only the scope claimed.
