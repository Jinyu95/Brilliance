Multi-bend achromat (MBA) storage-ring design

A one-request 3.5 GeV, 810 m, 32-cell modified-H7BA study (project-27a852b84dbd77). The saved ideal-ring baseline reaches 26.157 pm·rad, with sampled momentum survival to at least ±2.4% and passing sampled transverse checks under a provisional injected-beam assumption. Original conversation, plots and limitations; not an operationally qualified machine.

Exploring sub-10 pm storage-ring designs

A multi-turn exploration of two-arc superperiods (project-0df80d3fd5024f), including the latest T00/T02/T06 acceptance study and original design plots. Nominal emittances of 6.34–6.51 pm·rad accompany unresolved nonlinear-acceptance tradeoffs and numerical convergence. This is exploratory research—not a guarantee of reaching a sub-10 pm design or an engineering-qualified machine.

Space-charge structure resonance in a fixed 1 GeV FODO ring

A coasting proton beam's space-charge tune footprint is driven across the 4Qx = 18 structure resonance in a fixed-magnet ring, diagnosed with turn-by-turn emittance, phase space, and finite-time frequency-map (FMA) analysis (project-40c9bb49d1383e).

Real-machine optics identification with LOCO

A JuTrack-based study of HEPS orbit-response and dispersion measurements (project-48df51d11b2c79), with 39 full iterations in the final coupled-fit refinement, calibration diagnostics and verified JuTrack calculations. The offline fit converged, but residuals remain above the measurement resolution; no correction was applied to or validated on the machine.
